Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Cash Tin app collects, why, who it's shared with, and your rights. Cash Tin is made by Dynamics Development Solutions ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for information handled by our servers.

The short version

  • Volunteers don't need an account. Each phone signs in anonymously.
  • We don't show ads, we don't sell data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Card payments are taken by SumUp in the SumUp app. We never see card details.
  • Gift Aid declarations include a donor's name and address. In a shared event, only organisers can read them.

What we collect

Event and sales information

When you use Cash Tin, the app records the event name, join code, till names, the price list, each sale (items, amounts, cash or card, time) and each till's opening float. On a single phone this stays on the phone. In a shared event it's also stored on our servers so that every till can add up to one day total.

Gift Aid declarations

If an organiser turns on Gift Aid, donors can make a declaration on the till. This records the donor's first name, last name, house name or number, postcode, the donation amount and the date. It's collected for the charity or organisation running the event, so they can claim Gift Aid from HMRC. In a shared event, declarations are stored on our servers and can only be read by that event's organisers.

For Gift Aid, the organisation running the event is the data controller for donors' details, and we process them on its behalf. Donors should contact the organisation that ran the event about their declaration. We'll help that organisation with any request.

Anonymous sign-in

Each phone signs in to our servers anonymously. This gives the phone a random ID used to keep events secure. It isn't linked to your name or email.

Accounts (optional)

Organisers who buy event packs sign in with email and password, Google or Apple. We store your email address, your sign-in method and the number of event credits on your account. With Sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email address.

Purchases

Event packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check purchases and add credits, which receives your account ID and purchase details. We never receive your payment card details.

Card payments

Card payments are handed to the SumUp app and taken under the stall's own SumUp account. Cash Tin only stores the amount and SumUp's transaction reference, so the payment can be matched up later. SumUp's own privacy policy covers the payment itself.

How we use it

  • To run the till and sync sales between tills at your event.
  • To show day totals, summaries and exports to organisers.
  • To record Gift Aid declarations for the organisation running the event.
  • To manage accounts and event credits, and check purchases.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To answer you when you contact us for support.

Under UK GDPR, our legal bases are: providing the service you asked for (contract), our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and working, and, for Gift Aid, the event organisation's legitimate interest and legal obligation in claiming Gift Aid.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete the event from the app, clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Shared events: kept so organisers can look back at totals and Gift Aid records, which charities may need for HMRC for up to six years. Organisers can ask us to delete an event at any time.
  • Accounts: until you delete your account.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to help you, then deleted.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to how we use it, or ask us to limit its use. Email us at support@dynamicsdevelopment.co.uk and we'll reply within one month.

Deleting your account: in the app, open Account and tap Delete account. This deletes your sign-in and any unused event credits.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Cash Tin is meant to be used by adult volunteers and organisers. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Our server rules let each till see only its own event, and let only organisers read Gift Aid declarations or change the price list.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
