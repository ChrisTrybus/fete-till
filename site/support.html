Common questions

How do volunteers join an event?

The organiser starts the event and gets a 6-character join code on the Event tab. Each volunteer opens Cash Tin, chooses to join an event, enters the code and gives their till a name, such as "Cake stall". Volunteers don't need an account.

What happens if a till has no signal?

Keep selling. Every sale is saved on the phone before anything else, and waits in a queue. When the phone gets signal again, the queued sales are sent automatically and appear in the day total. Don't uninstall the app or clear its data while sales are still waiting to send.

How do I make another phone an organiser?

On the organiser's phone, open the Event tab and find the organiser code. Enter that code on the other phone. Organisers can change the price list, void any till's sales and close the event. Only share the code with people you trust.

I reinstalled the app or changed phone. How do I get back in?

Join the event again with its join code. If you were an organiser, enter the organiser code too and your organiser controls come back. If you bought event packs, sign in to the same account to get your credits back.

How do card payments work?

Cash Tin hands card payments to the SumUp app. Each stall uses its own SumUp account, and the payment is taken in SumUp on a SumUp reader or with Tap to Pay. The money goes straight to that SumUp account and never passes through Cash Tin. If SumUp doesn't report back, check the payment in the SumUp app before confirming it on the till.

How does Gift Aid work?

An organiser can turn on Gift Aid for the event. After a donation, the till offers the donor a declaration, which takes their name, house name or number, and postcode. Only organisers can see declarations, and they can export them to help with an HMRC Gift Aid claim. Gift Aid only applies to donations, not to things bought.

What are event packs?

Using Cash Tin on a single phone is free. To run an event across several tills, the organiser uses one credit from an event pack, bought in the app. Packs are linked to your Cash Tin account, so sign in before you buy.

My event pack purchase didn't add credits

Make sure you're signed in to the account you bought with, and check that the phone has signal. If credits still don't appear, email us with the date of purchase and the email address of your account. Please don't send card details.

How do I close the event and get the totals?

An organiser closes the event from the Event tab at the end of the day. All tills stop taking sales. Open Day total to see takings by till, by item, and by cash and card, then share the summary as text or PDF, or export a CSV.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

If you no longer have the app, email support@dynamicsdevelopment.co.uk from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your account removes your sign-in, email address and any unused event credits. To ask us to delete event data such as sales or Gift Aid declarations, email us too. See our privacy policy for more.

How do I get a refund for an event pack?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play, then Payments & subscriptions, then Budget & order history.
