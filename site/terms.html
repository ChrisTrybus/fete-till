Using Cash Tin

You can use Cash Tin without an account to run a till on your own phone, and to join an event that someone else has started. Please use it only for lawful fundraising and sales, and don't try to disrupt the service, get into other people's events, or get around how event packs work.

If you start an event, you decide who gets the join code and the organiser code. Anyone with the join code can add sales to the event, and anyone with the organiser code can change the price list, void sales and close the event. Only share them with people you trust.

Accounts

You need a Cash Tin account to buy and use event packs. Keep your sign-in details safe, and tell us if you think someone else is using your account. You can delete your account at any time in the app, under Account, or by emailing us. See how to delete your account.

Event packs and credits

  • Event packs are one-off purchases, not subscriptions. Each credit lets you start one shared event, with as many tills as you need.
  • A credit is used when you start the shared event, not when it closes.
  • Credits don't expire. They belong to the account you bought them with and can't be moved to another account, swapped for money, or sold on.
  • Buying any pack also unlocks saved price lists on that account for good.
  • If you delete your account, any unused credits are lost.
  • Prices are shown in the app before you buy, and may change. A change doesn't affect packs you've already bought.

Card payments at your event

Cash Tin hands card payments to the SumUp app. The payment is taken by SumUp under the stall's own SumUp account and SumUp's terms, and the money goes straight to that account. Cash Tin never handles the money or sees card details. Before confirming a card sale on the till, check that it went through in the SumUp app.

If you turn on Gift Aid, the charity or organisation running the event is responsible for its Gift Aid claim: checking that donations qualify, keeping declarations as HMRC requires, and handling donors' details lawfully.
