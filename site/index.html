A simple till for fetes and fairs

Volunteers tap what's being bought, enter the cash handed over, and Cash Tin shows the change. Every till at your event adds up to one shared day total.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Works with no signal

Every sale is saved on the phone first, then synced when there's signal. A dead spot behind the tombola never loses a sale.

Every till, one total

Start an event and share a 6-character join code. Volunteers join on their own phones, with no account needed.

One shared price list

Set prices once. Organisers can change them during the day, and every till picks up the changes.

Cash and card

Work out change for cash, or take cards through your own SumUp account. The money goes straight to you.

Gift Aid

Take Gift Aid declarations on the till after a donation, ready for your HMRC claim.

Easy end of day

Takings by till, by item, and by cash and card. Share a summary as text or PDF, or export a CSV for the treasurer.
